Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

PrimePickHub does not require user registration and does not process financial transactions. We may collect standard non-personally identifiable click analytics (referrer URL, browser type, and a truncated IP hash for security) to track outbound affiliate clicks and prevent abuse. We never sell your data.

2. Cookies & Third-Party Tracking

When you click on store links (Daraz, Amazon, AliExpress, eBay, etc.), third-party affiliate networks may set tracking cookies on your device to attribute commissions. These cookies are controlled by those networks, not by PrimePickHub. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

3. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate visitor behavior (pages visited, time on site, geographic region). Google Analytics collects anonymized data and does not personally identify you. You can opt out using Google Analytics Opt-Out.

4. Children's Privacy

PrimePickHub is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to: (a) request information about data we hold about you, (b) request deletion of any data we hold, and (c) opt out of analytics tracking by disabling cookies. Contact us at support@primepickhub.com for any privacy request.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, email us at support@primepickhub.com or visit our Contact Page.
