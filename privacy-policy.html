1. Information We Collect

PrimePickHub does not require user registration for browsing. However, if you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. When you submit a product review, we collect your name, review text, and any photos you upload. We use cookies to store your preferences and track basic visitor analytics. When you click on store links (Daraz, Amazon, AliExpress, etc.), third-party affiliate networks may set tracking cookies on your device to attribute commissions. These cookies are controlled by those networks. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

3. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate visitor behavior (pages visited, time on site, geographic region). Google Analytics collects anonymized data and does not personally identify you. You can opt out using Google Analytics Opt-Out.

4. Children's Privacy

PrimePickHub is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to: (a) request information about data we hold about you, (b) request deletion of any data we hold (e.g., newsletter email or review), and (c) opt out of analytics tracking by disabling cookies. Contact us at support.primepickhub@gmail.com for any privacy request.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, email us at support.primepickhub@gmail.com or visit our Contact Page.
